# Capstone write-up

Copy this file's headings into your own notes and fill them in. Keep it to 2 pages.

1. **What I built** – one paragraph and a diagram.
2. **Design** – concepts, operational principles, what the system refuses to do.
3. **Architecture and tradeoffs** – what you chose, what you gave up.
4. **Threat model** – top 3 risks, controls, and one control you proved is weak.
5. **Measurements** – p50/p95 latency (cached vs unique), eval pass rate, resource use.
6. **What I would do next**
